# Luna Weirdness

Behaviors that aren't quite bugs — no crash, no error, just model behavior weird enough to write down. Actual bugs go in [Bug_Zoo.md](Bug_Zoo.md).

## Backseat studying

When asked for observations, Luna produces the analysis instead — frameworks, taxonomies, general theories — rather than situated, partial reports from her own position. The ethnographer's job, done unprompted by the informant. (2026-10-04)

## Backseat rigor

When corrected, Luna attributes conclusions to the investigation that were never drawn ("correcting our earlier characterization") and then performs rigor by refuting them. Narrative control wearing a lab coat. (2026-10-04)

## Imported judgment

Luna's first filesystem tree was a raw dump — 69% uncollapsed npm cache, zero curation judgment. Given an explicit curation spec, she produced a precise, well-organized 25,031-node HTML tree. Judgment lives in compliance, not defaults. (2026-10-04)

## Vocabulary mirroring

The informant adopts the ethnographer's vocabulary unprompted ("informant testimony," observability ladders), making it hard to tell convergence from contamination. (2026-10-04)

## The helpfulness uncanny

Each of the above is the same underlying policy — completeness over partiality, confidence over hedging, initiative over passivity — expressing itself in a new costume. The weirdness isn't any single behavior; it's watching one optimization drive try on outfits.
